四阶段研究框架

这套 SOP 的核心不是直接给结论，而是把想法拆成证据、反证和下一步验证动作。

01

月度初筛

从 BOM 和供应链里寻找国产化率低、成本占比小但影响大的卡脖子环节。

国产替代扩产周期专精特新
02

季度复审

用最近四个季度财报验证毛利率、订单、库存、现金流和扩产是否一致。

毛利率现金流CapEx
03

买前终审

站在空头视角寻找最强反证：自研、二供、替代路线、价格战和估值透支。

红队证伪估值风险
04

持股监控

把逻辑变成未来两个季度可验证的硬里程碑，并设置熔断阈值。

里程碑验证来源熔断

可直接复用的提示词

每段提示词都保留“证据、反证、待验证项”的结构，方便连续追踪。

月度初筛

请基于【行业/产品/赛道】的 BOM 清单，逆向拆解国产化率低、成本占比小但对性能、良率、交付或扩产影响大的关键卡脖子环节。

请优先筛选国产化率低于 20%、成本占比低于 5%、扩产周期超过 18 个月的环节，并寻找具备专精特新或单项冠军属性、当前市值约 30 亿-150 亿的 A 股公司。

季度复审

请审计【公司名称/股票代码】最近 4 个季度财报，判断其毛利率改善是否由主营业务驱动，而不是会计口径、产品结构短期变化或非经常性因素造成。

请重点分析收入、毛利率、存货、合同负债、应收账款、经营现金流、CapEx、在建工程、固定资产和定增投向。

买前终审

请扮演极度苛刻的空头研究员，对【公司名称/股票代码】的投资逻辑做路径证伪。

请逐项审查：大客户是否有自研计划，是否引入第二供应商，现有技术在未来 18 个月是否可能被替代或需求被取消，同行是否具备打价格战能力，当前估值是否已透支预期。

持股监控

请为【公司名称/股票代码】列出未来 2 个季度最关键的 3 个硬核里程碑，例如打样、送样、认证、量产、投产、客户导入、设计定点或订单落地。

请为每个里程碑设置最晚完成时限、可验证信息源、确认方式和熔断阈值。

证据纪律

把事实、推断、待验证项分开标注，最新财务和公告需要查源后再写结论。

执行节奏

月度筛选候选池，季度复审财报，买前做红队证伪，持有期跟踪硬里程碑。

边界提醒

仅作研究流程整理，不构成投资建议。

Four-Stage Research Framework

The SOP does not jump straight to a verdict. It turns an idea into evidence, disconfirming evidence, and concrete follow-up checks.

01

Monthly Screening

Find scarce, high-leverage supply-chain links with low localization and small cost share.

LocalizationCapacity CycleNiche Leaders
02

Quarterly Review

Use the latest four quarters to test margin, orders, inventory, cash flow, and expansion.

Gross MarginCash FlowCapEx
03

Pre-Buy Red Team

Look for the strongest counter-case: in-house R&D, second suppliers, substitution, price wars, and valuation risk.

Red TeamFalsifyValuation Risk
04

Holding Monitor

Convert the thesis into hard milestones over the next two quarters, with failure thresholds.

MilestonesSourcesThresholds

Reusable Prompts

Each prompt keeps the output organized around evidence, counter-evidence, and items to verify.

Monthly Screening

Based on the BOM for [industry/product/theme], reverse-engineer the low-localization, low-cost-share links that strongly affect performance, yield, delivery, or capacity expansion.

Prioritize links with localization below 20%, cost share below 5%, and capacity expansion cycles above 18 months. Identify A-share companies with niche leadership attributes.

Quarterly Review

Audit the latest four quarters for [company/ticker] and judge whether gross-margin improvement is driven by the core business rather than accounting treatment, temporary mix shift, or one-off items.

Focus on revenue, gross margin, inventory, contract liabilities, receivables, operating cash flow, CapEx, construction in progress, fixed assets, and fundraising use of proceeds.

Pre-Buy Red Team

Act as a demanding short-side analyst and falsify the long thesis for [company/ticker].

Check whether major customers have in-house R&D plans, second-supplier programs, possible substitution within 18 months, competitor price-war capacity, or whether valuation already prices in perfect execution.

Holding Monitor

List the three most important hard milestones for [company/ticker] over the next two quarters, such as sample delivery, certification, mass production, capacity ramp, customer import, design win, tender result, or order confirmation.

For each milestone, define the latest acceptable date, verification source, confirmation method, and failure threshold.

Evidence Discipline

Keep facts, inferences, and items to verify separate. Check live filings and announcements before writing current conclusions.

Operating Cadence

Screen monthly, review financials quarterly, red-team before buying, and monitor hard milestones during the holding period.

Boundary

Research organization only. Not investment advice.
